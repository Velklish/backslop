# BS-247.13 · Make the init measurement note independent of an unnamed acceptance commit

- **Scope:** [01. Layout and formats](../../reference/01-layout.md)
- **Created:** 2026-10-22
- **Parent:** BS-247
- **Cost:** minor

## Context

The init reference points readers to an unnamed acceptance commit for its measurement record. Someone reading a package, snapshot or standalone reference cannot identify "this change" or the intended commit message.

Evidence was inspected at source HEAD `44e74a32ff6a6b6c946321b3614b1881a2ccedfe`.

## Evidence

- `docs/reference/01-layout.md:47`: "The record is the Checks paragraph of the message of the commit that accepts this change".
- `docs/README.md` repeats that sentence as a guarded statement.
- The surrounding paragraph already supplies the date, versions, command, the `--tools ""` condition, one run per condition, the observation and its limit. Those facts can be understood without the acceptance workflow.

The commit pointer is authoring-process metadata rather than a usable reference. The age or provenance of the measured observation is not itself the defect.

## Work to do

1. Remove the sentence directing the reader to an unnamed acceptance commit. Keep the current init behavior and the bounded measurement statement.
2. Update the corresponding quote block in `docs/README.md` in the same change.
3. If raw receipts are needed, use a stable accessible link to explicitly identified material. Creating a new measurement dossier is not required just to remove this sentence.

Acceptance: the paragraph is understandable without Git history or knowledge of the task that produced it; its guarded quote agrees; the observation's conditions and limits remain explicit.

## Out of scope

Do not turn "init writes or warns" into a claim that every agent harness actually loaded the files. Do not refresh or generalize the old measurement without new evidence, remove useful dates/versions, or claim current external harness-documentation verification.
